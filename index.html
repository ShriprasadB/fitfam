The short version. Fit Fam counts your steps and shares your progress with the people you are competing against. That is all it is for.

Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with advertisers, and there are no third-party trackers or analytics in the app. You can delete your account from inside it.

  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No location
  • Nothing sold
  • Delete anytime

1Who this is

Fit Fam is an iPhone app made and run by an individual developer, not a company. Questions about anything on this page can go to fitfamsupport@gmail.com, and they will be answered by the person who wrote the app.

2What Fit Fam collects

Only what a step-counting app between friends actually needs. Every item below exists to make some part of the app work — nothing is collected in case it turns out to be useful later.

Motion & Fitness

Fit Fam reads your step count from the iPhone's motion coprocessor, using a read-only interface. It cannot write steps, and no other app can write steps into it either — which is what makes the numbers hard to fake. If you decline the Motion & Fitness permission the app has nothing to count.

Alongside each stretch of walking the app stores what the hardware reported about it — distance, cadence, altitude gained, the iPhone's own classification of the activity, and whether the clock and the hardware agreed. This is used to work out whether steps were really walked, so that challenges are fair. This detailed record stays on your iPhone and is not uploaded; what reaches your challenges is your step total.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If — and only if — you turn on Apple Watch calories in Profile, Fit Fam asks permission to read one thing from Apple Health: active energy, the calories an Apple Watch measured from your heart rate. It is used to show a better calorie figure than the app can estimate from steps alone.

Active energy is the only Health data type the app ever requests. Access is read-only: Fit Fam never writes anything to Apple Health. The figure is used on your iPhone to work out one number on screen — it is never uploaded, never stored against your account, and never shown to anyone else in your challenges. Turning the switch off stops the reading and discards what was loaded, and declining it changes nothing else in the app.

Health data is never used to decide a challenge. Challenges are scored on steps, which always come from the motion chip.

Information you enter yourself

On a treadmill you can tell the app the incline, because a moving belt gains no height and no sensor can detect it. That figure affects your calorie estimate only — it cannot change your steps, your distance, or anything a challenge is decided on — and it stays on your iPhone with the rest of your activity history.

3What Fit Fam does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers. The app shows no ads and does not ask for permission to track you across other apps or websites, because it has no use for it.
  • No selling or sharing. Your data is not sold, rented, or passed to data brokers.
  • No third-party analytics. There is no analytics SDK in the app.
  • No location. Fit Fam does not ask for or use GPS. Distance is worked out from your steps and your stride, which is why it keeps working with the phone in a pocket and no signal.
  • No writing to Apple Health. Fit Fam can read one figure from Health if you ask it to, and it puts nothing back. Your Health record is not changed by using this app.

4Who can see what

Fit Fam is a social app, so some of what it holds is deliberately visible to other people — but only to the people in a challenge with you.

  • People in your challenges see your name, your avatar, your step total, the distance and days you have logged, and anything you post in that challenge's chat. They also see how many of your steps failed the honesty checks, because that is what makes the competition credible.
  • Nobody else can read a challenge they are not part of. This is enforced by the database's own security rules, not only by the app.
  • Your body details are yours alone. Height, weight and sex are readable only by your own account.

5Where the data is kept

Fit Fam uses Firebase, run by Google LLC, for sign-in, storage and syncing between phones. Your data is stored on Google's servers under that service, and Google processes it on the app's behalf. Google's own handling of it is described in the Firebase privacy documentation.

Steps and history are also kept on your own iPhone, so the app works without a connection.

Some things never leave it at all: the detailed record of each stretch of walking, any treadmill incline you declared, and anything read from Apple Health are held on your iPhone only and are not sent to Firebase or anywhere else.

Data is kept for as long as you have an account. There is no fixed retention period, because a challenge from last year is part of your history until you decide otherwise.

6Reporting and blocking

If you report a message, a copy of that message and the accounts involved are sent to the developer so it can be looked at. Reports cannot be read by anyone using the app — not even by the person who filed one — and the person reported is not told.

Blocking someone is private to you. They are not notified, and it does not remove them from a challenge or change anyone's score.

7Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account → Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is removed:

  • Your profile, including your body details
  • Your sign-in, so the account can never be used again
  • Your name and avatar, everywhere they were copied — every challenge and every message you posted
  • Every photo you sent
  • Everything the app stored on your iPhone

What deliberately stays:

  • The step totals and results of challenges you took part in, and the text of messages you posted — all shown under the name “Deleted user” rather than yours.

This is a considered choice rather than an oversight. People in Fit Fam agree to real stakes with each other — the loser owes a chore. If deleting an account erased the numbers a result was worked out from, deleting would become a way out of a bet you had just lost, and it would rewrite a result other people are still looking at. So deletion removes who you were and keeps what was decided.

If you want something removed that this does not cover, write to fitfamsupport@gmail.com.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask what is held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Deletion is built into the app; for anything else, email fitfamsupport@gmail.com and it will be dealt with.

9Children

Fit Fam is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. The app carries chat and photo sharing between the people in a challenge, which is why it is rated for older users. If you believe a child has an account, email the address above and it will be removed.
